Non-profit partnership

What we ask of IHA, and what IHA receives.

What we ask

  1. Permission to use collection assets in Jalana: photos, collection data, 3D models and stories. To begin, 10 collection items per museum.
  2. Access to the documentation of 19 artefacts for Arcantara: 8 repatriated statues and 11 from the MCB Masterpiece Collection. The list is in the Arcantara section.
  3. A trial at selected IHA museums, with check-in booths and QR labels. We report the results to IHA.

What IHA receives

  • Visit data per museum and per period, including room reach.
  • Digital stamps for Museum Passport, and a booth where book owners stamp their passports.
  • A learning channel for junior and senior high schools: stories, 360°, AR and a restoration game.
  • Collections that become more widely known through shared cards, badges and artefacts.

Assets remain IHA's property. IHA curators review the content. IHA can ask for an asset to be replaced or taken down at any time.

From meeting to trial

Jalana needs permission to use collection assets. We start small: 10 collection items per museum.

  1. Week 1

    Meeting and demo

    A demo of Jalana and Arcantara. IHA names a contact person.

  2. Weeks 2 to 4

    Letter of agreement

    Trial permission, asset permission and the list of 10 collection items per museum are agreed in one letter.

  3. Weeks 5 to 8

    Assets and curation

    The first batch of assets is handed over. IHA curators review the cards and stories. The booths are prepared.

  4. After preparation

    2-month trial

    Jalana runs at selected IHA museums. Its visit data is reported to IHA.

  5. After the trial

    Joint review

    The trial results decide the next steps for Jalana's public release and Arcantara's development.
